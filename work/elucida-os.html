Elucida OS

What it is

A security-focused, immutable, container-native Linux distribution built on Fedora Kinoite (bootc). A kill-chain-organized tool launcher, a scope-aware terminal, and a bring-your-own-key AI workflow layer.

Where it is

An early, OSS-first preview — open to collaborators who want a reproducible, immutable base for offensive work.
