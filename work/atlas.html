What it is

ATLAS is a fully-local, voice-activated AI companion — a real personality with real hands and real memory. You just talk to it. The brain, speech, vision and memory all run on your own GPU. No cloud, no API keys, nothing leaves the machine.

Why it feels alive

Full-duplex voice with real barge-in — interrupt it mid-sentence. It carries memory across sessions, reads your webcam and your screen, and actually operates the machine: types, clicks buttons by name, launches apps, drives its own browser. Measured at ~90 ms to first token and ~88 ms to first audio on a single RTX 5060 Ti.
